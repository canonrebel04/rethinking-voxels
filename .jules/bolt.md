## 2024-05-24 - GLSL Optimize clamp with FMA and Saturate
**Learning:** In GLSL shaders, division by constants and clamping bounds `[0.0, 1.0]` can be heavily optimized. Divisions should be changed to multiplication, and expressions of the form `clamp(x + c, 0.0, max) / max` can be transformed to `clamp((x/max) + (c/max), 0.0, 1.0)`. This structure replaces the computationally expensive division with a Fused Multiply-Add (FMA) operation and a free `saturate` instruction. For example: `clamp(SdotU + 0.375, 0.0, 0.75) / 0.75` becomes `clamp(SdotU * 1.3333333 + 0.5, 0.0, 1.0)`.
**Action:** When finding patterns of `clamp(x + const, 0.0, max_val) / max_val`, convert them into the `clamp(x * (1.0 / max_val) + (const / max_val), 0.0, 1.0)` format, and append the `/* Opt: FMA + saturate */` comment to meet Bolt persona requirements and avoid overhead of costly division.
