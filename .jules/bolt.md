## 2024-10-19 - FMA Optimization on Clamp + Division Expressions
**Learning:** In GLSL shaders, division is computationally more expensive than multiplication. Expressions structured like `clamp(x + c, 0.0, max_val) / max_val` can be mathematically simplified to `clamp(x * (1.0/max_val) + (c/max_val), 0.0, 1.0)`. Not only does this replace division with multiplication, but optimizing to a `[0.0, 1.0]` clamp range enables the compiler to use a free `saturate` instruction modifier.
**Action:** When identifying `clamp` and division operations in GLSL code, look for opportunities to pre-calculate reciprocals to multiply inside the clamp instead, especially aiming for a `0.0, 1.0` range to leverage the hardware `saturate` modifier. Always append `/* Opt: FMA + saturate */` to document this inline.
