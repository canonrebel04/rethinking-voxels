## 2024-06-25 - GLSL pow(x, 2) optimization
**Learning:** In GLSL, using `pow(x, 2.0)` might be safe from macro double-evaluation, but extracting complex expressions into a local variable and multiplying it by itself (e.g., `float t = max(...); result = t * t;`) is universally safe and effectively equivalent, bypassing potential issues since some compilers may not fully optimize `pow()` despite general capabilities. Type compatibility and shader safety should also be maintained by explicitly casting integers or using float literals (e.g. `0.0`).
**Action:** When optimizing `pow(x, 2)` or `pow(x, 2.0)` calls, explicitly extract the expression to a local variable `t` and replace the function call with `t * t` to ensure safety and compiler-independent performance improvements.
