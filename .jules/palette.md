## 2024-05-18 - Menu Organization for Orphaned Settings
**Learning:** In Minecraft shaderpacks (which lack traditional web UIs), options configured in `.lang` files but missing from `shaders.properties` screen mappings are entirely inaccessible to users. Grouping a related toggle (like `WORLD_OUTLINE`) and its associated, previously orphaned slider (`WORLD_OUTLINE_THICKNESS`) into a logical sub-menu (`[WORLD_OUTLINE_SETTINGS]`) is an effective micro-UX improvement. It not only restores access to the missing setting but also cleans up the top-level menu layout by substituting multiple root options with a single menu reference.
**Action:** When auditing shader UIs, cross-reference `.lang` options against `shaders.properties` screen hierarchies to find orphaned settings, and group them with their parent toggles in new sub-menus rather than simply appending them to crowded root screens.
