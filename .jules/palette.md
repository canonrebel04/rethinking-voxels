## 2024-07-19 - Shader Options UX
**Learning:** Grouping related toggle options and their sliders into sub-menus (using `[MENU_NAME]`) within `shaders.properties` cleans up top-level menus and exposes previously inaccessible, orphaned settings (like `WORLD_OUTLINE_THICKNESS`).
**Action:** Cross-reference `.lang` options with `.properties` screens to find orphaned options and group them logically under existing screens.
