## 2024-07-24 - Uncovering Hidden Settings
**Learning:** In Minecraft shaderpacks, orphaned `.lang` localization entries often indicate a setting that exists but needs to be explicitly added to the property tree in `shaders.properties` to become accessible to users in the UI.
**Action:** When working on shaderpack UX, cross-reference `.lang` files with `shaders.properties` screen mappings to uncover and expose missing accessibility or configuration options.
